    About this thing

    This is my basic training journal. I wrote it in a notebook at Fort Jackson between January 12 and March 25, 2026, mostly in two-to-ten minute windows at the end of sixteen hour days, or on fire guard at one in the morning. A lot of it was dictated with voice-to-text by a guy who was cold, starving and running on five hours of sleep.

    What was and wasn't changed

    • The words are untouched. Nothing was rewritten, cleaned up, or corrected. The mangled voice-to-text is part of the record.
    • Paragraph breaks were added so it's readable on a screen. That's the only formatting liberty taken.
    • Page titles and chapter names were added for navigation. They aren't in the notebook.
    • Dates marked ≈ are estimates — inferred from what the entry says about the day. Dates without it were written on the page. Where the notebook has an obvious slip (a "February 2" entry sitting in the middle of February 11), the original heading is kept and the estimate sits beside it.
    • The rough parts stayed in. Slurs, the passage about race in the barracks, the parts about other trainees' worst days. It's a private journal written by somebody in a bad state, not a publication. Editing it after the fact would make it a different document.

Every quoted line under an answer is checked against the source at build time, so if a quote is there, it's really in the notebook on that page.

    The drawings

    There are — drawings noted in the journal that aren't scanned in yet. They show up as empty slots with the slot name on them, like p5-1. Drop the image into images/ and add a line to data/art.js and it'll appear in place.

    Names

    Everyone here is as they appear in the notebook — mostly last names, a few nicknames, one or two people who exist under three different spellings because of voice-to-text. Karagach and Arthur are the same guy. Hackerman is Herman. Cult rider, colt and Kaltrider are all Kaltrider.

    How it's built

    Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step required to view it — open index.html. The journal text lives in source/journal.txt and the questions in source/questions.txt; both are plain text. Run python3 tools/build.py and python3 tools/build_questions.py after editing either one.
